\section{Evolution of the VaR (Point 1, comment)}\label{sec:dynamics}

Figures~\ref{fig:var_SMI}--\ref{fig:var_UBS} show the 1\% VaR of three groups of models for the SMI, the EWP and
UBS (the 5\% VaR is in the Appendix). Tables~\ref{tab:dyn_SMI}--\ref{tab:dyn_UBS} summarise the dynamics: spikes
(largest one-day change), staleness (share of days on which the VaR does not change) and how fast the VaR follows
the realised volatility.
